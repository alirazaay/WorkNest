* Remaining leave balance
* Payslip questions
* Company policies
* Holidays
* Benefits

---

### AI Resume Parser

Automatically extracts:

* Name
* Skills
* Experience
* Education
* Contact Info

---

### AI Candidate Ranking

Ranks applicants based on:

* Skills
* Experience
* Job Description
* Interview Scores

---

### AI Attendance Insights

Detects:

* Habitual lateness
* Absenteeism
* Overtime trends
* Attendance anomalies

---

### AI Payroll Checker

Detects:

* Incorrect deductions
* Salary anomalies
* Missing attendance
* Duplicate payments

---

### AI Performance Insights

Suggests:

* Promotions
* Training
* Skill gaps
* Career paths

---

### AI HR Analytics

Ask questions naturally:

> "Show departments with the highest turnover."

> "Who worked the most overtime this month?"

> "Employees with low attendance but high performance."

---

### AI Document Generator

Generate:

* Offer Letters
* Experience Letters
* Warning Letters
* Promotion Letters
* Salary Certificates

---

### AI Policy Assistant

Upload company policies.

Employees ask:

> "Can I carry forward annual leave?"

AI answers using company documents.
